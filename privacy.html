Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Panne (“Panne”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our note-taking and study companion at panne.app.

Information we collect

When you create an account, Firebase Authentication may process your email address, display name, profile image, authentication provider, and a unique account identifier. When you use Panne, we process the notebooks, pages, drawings, flashcards, and other content you choose to save.

If you use lecture dictation, your browser’s speech-recognition service may process microphone audio according to that provider’s terms. When you upload audio or video for transcription, the file is sent to our transcription provider for processing. When you submit a public video URL, we process the URL and publicly available caption data needed to create notes.

How we use information

  • To provide, secure, and improve Panne.
  • To authenticate accounts and synchronize notes across devices.
  • To create transcripts and summaries you request.
  • To respond to support requests and prevent abuse.
  • To operate subscriptions or advertising if those features are enabled.

Storage and retention

Notes are stored in Firebase Firestore under your account. Local browser storage may also keep an offline copy. Uploaded media is transmitted for the requested transcription and is not intended to be used as a permanent media library. We retain account and note data while your account is active or as needed to provide the service.

Third-party services

Panne uses Firebase for authentication and database services, Cloudflare for hosting and security, and transcription or video-caption providers when you request those features. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Your choices

You may access, export, or delete your notes through the app. To request account deletion or ask a privacy question, contact support@panne.app. We may need to verify ownership of the account before completing a request.

Children

Panne is not directed to children under the minimum age required in their country. Do not use the service if you are not legally permitted to do so.

Changes

We may update this policy as Panne changes. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the effective date.
