PANNE SUPPORT

Contact us

We usually reply within two business days.

Need help with your account, a transcription, a billing question, or a privacy request? Email us with enough detail to investigate. Please do not send passwords, API keys, or sensitive personal information.

General supportsupport@panne.app
Privacy requestsprivacy@panne.app

Include this information

  • The email address on your Panne account.
  • The page or feature where the problem occurred.
  • The approximate time and device/browser you used.
  • A screenshot or error message, with private data removed.

For account deletion, write “Account deletion request” in the subject line. We may ask you to verify account ownership.
